# BotGTAVI – Privacy Policy (TikTok)

*Last updated: November 16, 2026*

## 1. Who we are
BotGTAVI is a personal desktop tool used only by its developer, who is also the owner of the TikTok account @valcardipachi and of the YouTube channel "TODO SOBRE GTA VI". It creates short videos about the video game Grand Theft Auto VI and publishes them to the developer's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public. Contact: valcarofficial@gmail.com.

## 2. TikTok data we access
When the account owner logs in with TikTok (Login Kit), BotGTAVI receives only:

- Basic profile information (user.info.basic): open_id, display name and avatar, used only to confirm which account is connected.
- Permission to publish videos (video.publish / video.upload) to that same account through the Content Posting API.

BotGTAVI does not read followers, messages, comments, likes, viewing history or any data from other TikTok users.

## 3. How we use it
The data is used exclusively to publish the videos the account owner creates to the account owner's own TikTok profile, and to check the publishing status of those videos. It is never used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

## 4. Storage and security
The TikTok access token is stored only on the account owner's own computer, in a local file that is not shared or uploaded anywhere. No TikTok data is stored on servers, sold, rented or shared with third parties.

## 5. Retention and deletion
The token is kept only while publishing is active. The owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions) and delete the local token file; after that BotGTAVI keeps no TikTok data. Published videos can be deleted from the TikTok app.

## 6. Children
BotGTAVI is not directed to children and does not process data of any user other than its adult owner.

## 7. Changes
This policy may be updated; the "Last updated" date shows the current version.
